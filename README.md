# Top Set

A strength log for your phone: prefilled sets, one-tap logging, sticking-point tags, plateau checks, accessory picks, charts, and bar-math tools.

**Open it:** https://dreamywizard2502.github.io/Top-Set/

On an iPhone, open the link in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.

- No account needed. Your log is saved on your phone only.
- Back it up now and then with **Settings → Export JSON**. **Import JSON** restores it on a new phone.
- Works without a connection after the first visit.

The files here are generated from a separate source repository. Edits made directly here are overwritten by the next update.
